# Customer Support FAQs

This document grounds the FAQ agent's answers. The support team can add, edit, or
remove entries at any time — the agent reads it live on every request. To add a
new FAQ, copy an existing `### Q:` / `A:` pair under the right topic.

## Shipping & Delivery

### Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping (1–2 business days)
is available at checkout. Delivery times may be longer during sale periods.

### Q: How much does shipping cost?
A: Standard shipping is free on orders over $50; otherwise it's a flat $4.99.
Express shipping is $12.99.

### Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to most countries. International delivery takes 7–14 business days
and customs fees may apply depending on your location.

### Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships you'll receive a tracking link by email. You can also
view live status in the chat by giving your order id (e.g. ORD-1001).

## Returns & Refunds

### Q: What is your return policy?
A: Most items can be returned within 30 days of the order date for a refund.
Final-sale items (clearance, gift cards, perishables) cannot be returned, and
items damaged by the customer after delivery are not eligible.

### Q: How do I request a refund?
A: Tell the assistant your order id and the reason for the return. We'll check it
against the refund policy and let you know the outcome.

### Q: When will I get my money back?
A: Once a refund is approved, it is issued after we receive the returned item.
Refunds typically appear on your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

### Q: Do I have to pay for return shipping?
A: Returns are free for defective or incorrect items. For change-of-mind returns,
a $4.99 return-shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## Orders

### Q: Can I change or cancel my order?
A: Orders can be changed or cancelled while they are still "processing." Once an
order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you may be able to return it.

### Q: I received the wrong item — what do I do?
A: We're sorry about that. Start a refund or replacement in the chat with your
order id; wrong-item returns are free and prioritized.

### Q: An item is missing from my order.
A: If part of your order is missing, contact us with your order id and we'll
investigate and ship the missing item or refund it.

## Payments

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), as well
as PayPal and Apple Pay.

### Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes. Payments are processed by a PCI-compliant provider; we never store your
full card number on our servers.

## Account & Products

### Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click "Sign Up" at checkout or in the top menu and enter your email. An account
lets you track orders and manage returns.

### Q: How do I find the right size?
A: Each product page has a size guide with measurements. If you're between sizes,
we generally recommend sizing up. Sizing-related returns are accepted within the
30-day window.

### Q: Is an item in stock?
A: Product availability is shown on each product page. Out-of-stock items show an
estimated restock date when available.
